Finnegans Wake

James Joyce, Finnegans Wake. This deck is the text of the book, one card per printed page, one fragment per printed line, made to be read slowly and annotated. Each line shows its identifier, the page and the line number (p003-04 is the line 3.04); this is how notes and links refer to it.

Pages

– Contents: every page, by book and chapter.

– The whole book, page after page: series book (or all at once, flat).

– Book I: I.1, I.2, I.3, I.4, I.5, I.6, I.7, I.8.

– Book II: II.1, II.2, II.3, II.4.

– Book III: III.1, III.2, III.3, III.4.

– Book IV.

– The first page: 3.

About the text

– Editions: the printings and transcriptions used, how the text was established, and the form of the apparatus of variants (the footnotes that record where the first edition, 1939, and the corrected Viking printing, 1958, differ).

– Tools: the programs that establish the text and generate the deck.

– Glosses: the notes "ט word" in the right margin link to the glosses of finwake.com, a glossary of Finnegans Wake, where the word is explained.

– Notebooks: the notes "Ω" in the right margin link a phrase to the entry of Joyce's notebooks from which it comes, on the James Joyce Digital Archive (click the phrase there to see the entry).

– Readings: a footnote "ק" on the line where a recorded reading starts links to the recording at that line: James Joyce's own reading of the end of I.8 (1929), and the reading of Books I and II.1 by A Poetry Channel.
